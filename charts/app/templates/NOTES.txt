Ingress is disabled. Reach the service from inside the cluster, or port-forward it:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward \
    svc/{{ include "app.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then:
  curl http://localhost:8080/
  curl http://localhost:8080/healthz
  curl http://localhost:8080/readyz
  curl http://localhost:8080/metrics
{{- end }}

Check rollout status:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/{{ include "app.fullname" . }}
